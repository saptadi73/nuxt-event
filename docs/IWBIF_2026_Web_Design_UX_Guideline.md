# IWBIF 2026 Web Design & UX Guideline

**International Women Business & Investment Forum (IWBIF) 2026**\
**Theme:** *Empowering Women Entrepreneurs Through Finance, Global
Collaboration, and Digital Transformation*\
**Date:** 14--17 October 2026\
**Venue:** Hotel Kempinski Indonesia, Jakarta

## 1. Tujuan Dokumen

Dokumen ini mengembangkan panduan desain awal menjadi blueprint UI/UX
dan frontend untuk portal IWBIF 2026. Portal harus tampil sebagai
platform forum bisnis internasional yang premium, terpercaya, modern,
dan berorientasi pada koneksi bisnis nyata.

## 2. Design Direction

Gunakan pendekatan hybrid: - **Minimalist Executive & High-Tech** untuk
kesan premium dan modern. - **Interactive Narrative & Immersive
Storytelling** untuk hero dan storytelling. - **Data-Driven Enterprise &
Trust-Centric** untuk statistik, kredibilitas, partner, dan dashboard.

Positioning visual: \> **Premium International Business Forum ×
Executive Luxury × Global Investment Network**

Karakter: executive, global, elegant, technology-enabled, trustworthy,
inclusive, business & investment focused.

## 3. Brand & Visual System

### Warna

-   Deep Navy: `#04152D`, `#071D3A`, `#0B2447`
-   Premium Gold: `#C9963B`, `#D8AC59`, `#E6C477`
-   Warm White: `#F8F6F1`
-   Soft Gray: `#CBD2DC`
-   Dark Slate: `#15243A`

Gold digunakan sebagai aksen, bukan warna dominan pada area besar.

### Typography

Rekomendasi: - Display/editorial: **Cormorant Garamond** atau **Playfair
Display** - UI/body: **Plus Jakarta Sans**, **Inter**, atau **Geist**

Kombinasi utama: **Cormorant Garamond + Plus Jakarta Sans**.

## 4. Photography Direction

business matching, conference panels, professional networking,
Indonesian cultural elegance, Jakarta, dan international diversity.

focal point, dikelilingi women leaders internasional, skyline Jakarta,
peta Asia/Indonesia transparan, connection lines, dan adegan business
matching.

## 27. Bento Grid & Glassmorphism

Bento Grid cocok untuk statistics, benefits, dashboard summaries,
matching opportunities, Why Indonesia, dan event experience.

Glassmorphism digunakan selektif pada navbar, hero floating cards,
statistic cards, dan dashboard quick cards.

Pastikan contrast tetap memenuhi accessibility.

## 28. Micro-Interactions

Gunakan: - Gentle button scale - Arrow movement - Subtle border glow -
Card elevation - 2--4% image zoom - Animated navigation underline -
Count-up statistics sekali - Connection animation untuk business
matching

Animasi harus memperkuat kualitas, bukan mengganggu.

## 29. Scroll Storytelling

Gunakan hanya pada section bernilai tinggi.

### Global Connection

`Indonesia → Asia → Global Network`

### Business Matching

`PROFILE → DISCOVER → MATCH → MEET → DEAL`

### Participant Journey

`REGISTER → PAY → CONNECT → MATCH → MEET → DEAL`

Hindari scroll hijacking.

## 30. Responsive & Accessibility

Prioritas visual desktop, tetapi seluruh workflow transaksi wajib
mobile-friendly.

Sangat penting untuk: - Registration - Payment - QR Code - Schedule -
Messaging - Business Matching - Meeting confirmation

Target minimum: - Semantic HTML - Keyboard navigation - Visible focus
state - Sufficient contrast - Alt text - Accessible forms -
Reduced-motion support

## 31. Performance

Premium tidak boleh berarti lambat.

Gunakan: - Responsive images - WebP/AVIF - Lazy loading - SVG icons -
Font optimization - Code splitting - Dynamic import - Minimal animation
libraries - Image CDN bila tersedia - Skeleton loading

Hero image harus memiliki mobile-specific crop/asset.

## 33. Motion Rules

Durasi umum: - Hover: 150--250 ms - Card transition: 200--300 ms -
Section reveal: 400--700 ms - Page transition: 250--450 ms

Gunakan `transform` dan `opacity` sebisa mungkin. Hormati
`prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 34. SEO & International Event Discoverability

Setiap halaman publik perlu: - Unique title - Meta description - Open
Graph image - Canonical URL - Structured heading hierarchy - Event
structured data bila relevan - Speaker/exhibitor indexable pages sesuai
privacy policy

## 35. Design Do / Don't

### DO

-   Gunakan whitespace.
-   Gunakan navy/gold secara konsisten.
-   Jadikan business matching sebagai differentiator.
-   Gunakan real event/business imagery.
-   Buat CTA jelas.
-   Pertahankan hierarchy.
-   Optimalkan mobile.
-   Gunakan animation secara selektif.

### DON'T

-   Jangan membuat semua section beranimasi.
-   Jangan menggunakan gold berlebihan.
-   Jangan memakai terlalu banyak gradient.
-   Jangan memenuhi hero dengan terlalu banyak teks.
-   Jangan membuat dashboard seperti landing page.
-   Jangan menyembunyikan CTA utama.
-   Jangan mengorbankan performance untuk efek visual.

## 37. Final Design Principle

IWBIF 2026 harus terasa seperti **digital gateway menuju international
women business and investment ecosystem**, bukan hanya website informasi
event.

Formula desain:

> **Premium Visual + Global Credibility + Clear Information + Business
> Matching + Frictionless Registration + Action-Oriented Participant
> Dashboard**

Setiap halaman harus membantu pengguna melakukan salah satu dari empat
hal:

> **Discover → Connect → Match → Create Opportunity**
