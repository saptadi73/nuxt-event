# **3\. About the Event**

**The International Women Business & Investment Forum 2026 (IWBIF 2026\)**

The International Women Business & Investment Forum 2026 (IWBIF 2026\) is an international business forum that brings together women entrepreneurs from various countries within a trusted and curated collaboration ecosystem. The official support of the Government of the Republic of Indonesia, reflected through invitations extended to Embassies and representatives of partner countries, provides strong legitimacy to the networks and partnerships established through the forum. The forum serves as a safe platform for women entrepreneurs, particularly Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), to explore cross-border trade and investment opportunities in a more transparent and accountable manner. By combining high-level forums, business and investment matching sessions, international exhibitions, and a dedicated Youth Womenpreneur track, the event fosters the development of secure, inclusive, and sustainable women-led business collaborations. It is open to participation from countries across diverse regions of the world, creating opportunities for meaningful partnerships, market expansion, and long-term economic growth.

## **Why Indonesia?**

·       **A Market of Significant Scale**  
A large domestic market of more than 283 million people, with strong regional relevance for business and investment discussions  
·       **A Gateway for Cross-Border Collaboration**  
Strategically located between the Indian and Pacific Oceans, supporting ASEAN connectivity and broader Asian regional engagement.  
·       **A Strong Global Convening Power**  
As a G20 economy, Indonesia provides an ideal setting for public-private dialogue and practical deal-making opportunities.  
·       **Jakarta \= Business Deals \+ Diplomacy in One City**  
Investors, corporations, embassies, and decision-makers gather in one place for targeted business matching and MoU signings.  
·       **A Delegate – Ready Experience**  
**5**\-star package accommodation with twin-sharing rooms designed to encourage networking, including a welcome dinner, forum dinner, and selected Jakarta city tours
